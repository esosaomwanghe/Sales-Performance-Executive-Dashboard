# Sales Performance Executive Dashboard 💰

**Live Power BI Dashboard**  
Comprehensive retail sales performance report tracking $437K+ in revenue across 20+ Indian cities, 3 product categories, and 12-month trends — designed for regional managers and executives.

### Key Business Metrics (Full Year)
- **Total Sales**: **$437,770**  
- **Total Profit**: **$113,120**  
- **Profit Margin**: **26%**  
- **Top Performing City**: Indore ($62K)  
- **Best Category**: Clothing (highest volume & margin)

### Dashboard Highlights
- Monthly sales trend with clear seasonality (peak in October–December)
- Top 5 cities contributing ~55% of total revenue
- Sales breakdown by city and product category (Clothing • Electronics • Furniture)
- Interactive filters: Month, City, Category
- Clean, executive-ready design with conditional formatting

### Key Insights Delivered
- Clothing dominates in Tier-2 cities (Indore, Mumbai, Pune)
- Strong Q4 surge — ideal for inventory & marketing planning
- Low performers identified: Southern & Northeastern cities under $5K

### Tools & Techniques
- **Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX calculations (Total Profit, Margin %)
- **Power Query** – ETL, date table creation, data cleaning
- **Visual Best Practices** – Card visuals, conditional formatting, drill-through ready
